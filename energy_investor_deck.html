Proposed Energy Device — Investor Brief

🚀 Product Overview

We are building a smart energy device tailored for Indian homes. It enables efficient electricity usage, real-time consumption tracking, and intelligent insights to reduce both power bills and environmental impact. The plug-and-play device fits seamlessly with existing meters and appliances.

💼 Business Model

  • 🔧 Device sold at ₹4,500 to households and small businesses
  • 📊 Freemium analytics + Pro subscriptions available
  • 🌍 Primary focus: Tier 2/3 cities and rural energy users
  • 🏭 Made-in-India hardware + app ecosystem

📊 Unit Economics

  • Cost to manufacture: ₹3,000
  • Selling price: ₹4,500
  • Gross profit per unit: ₹1,500 (~33%)
  • Breakeven expected at 10,000 units

💰 Funding Requirements

We are raising ₹3 Crores to support:

  • 🧪 R&D, prototyping and design testing
  • ⚙️ Low-volume manufacturing & certifications
  • 📢 Initial marketing and channel partnerships
  • 📱 App development and customer onboarding

📈 Go-To-Market Strategy

  • ✅ Phase 1: Pilot deployment in gated communities and apartments
  • 🏘️ Partner with solar installers and RWAs for bundled offerings
  • 📲 Hyperlocal WhatsApp campaigns + Demo vans
  • 📦 Integrate with state subsidy/green programs

⚠️ Risks & Mitigation

  • Certification Delays: BIS/ISI/CE testing may take months
    🛡️ Mitigation: Parallel compliance initiation during prototyping
  • Price Sensitivity: Competing low-cost imports
    🛡️ Mitigation: Value-based selling, rural targeting, government tie-ups
  • Tech Adoption Lag: Households may resist new devices
    🛡️ Mitigation: Easy-to-use UI, demos, influencer-backed videos
  • After-Sales Support: Need for service across geographies
    🛡️ Mitigation: Community agents + WhatsApp bot-based support
