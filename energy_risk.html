Risk Mitigation Strategy

Market Risk

Concern: Will customers pay ₹4,500? Is there enough demand for 50K units/year?

Mitigation:

  • Do small pilot before scale
  • Partner with distributors
  • Validate price point with early adopters

Execution Risk

Concern: Can you really manufacture 50K/year and deliver consistently?

Mitigation:

  • Build buffer in supply chain
  • Start with 10K/year, scale later
  • Establish multiple manufacturing partners

R&D Uncertainty

Concern: Product may face tech or performance challenges

Mitigation:

  • Break R&D into milestones
  • Raise ₹3 Cr in tranches
  • Engage technical advisors early

Funding Risk

Concern: May need more funds before reaching breakeven

Mitigation:

  • Build in buffer for 18 months runway
  • Keep lean team
  • Establish clear KPIs for follow-on funding
