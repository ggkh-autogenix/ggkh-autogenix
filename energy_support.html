Competitive Landscape & Differentiation for Yan3

Competitor Analysis

Emporia Vue (Gen-3)

Imported

Type: Wi-Fi energy monitor with 3-phase + appliance sensors

Price: ₹21–30K in India (imported)

Limitations:

  • Separate sensor add-ons for appliance-level data
  • Imported; limited local support
  • Only modest savings (10-15%)

Mavaol / Generic DIN-Rail Smart Meters

Industrial

Type: DIN-rail industrial-style smart meters

Price: ₹26K–27K with duties

Limitations:

  • Not plug-and-play for homes
  • No per-device data or smart insights

Iskraemeco MT260

Utility-grade

Type: Utility-grade smart meter

Strengths: Certified, grid-integrated

Limitations:

  • Not aimed at households
  • No direct consumer UI or app

Enterprise Solutions (Fluentgrid, SATEC)

B2B Only

Focus: Grid analytics and industrial metering

Limitations:

  • B2B-only; no household applications
  • Expensive, complex integration
